CLOUD READINESS FOR SMEs

Should your business move to the cloud?

A cloud move can make sense — but not everything should move, and not every business needs the same answer. CloudFIXER helps you understand the business case before you commit to a migration.

Backed by experienced AWS and cloud professionals. Our recommendations are grounded in real architecture, migration and operational experience, with the focus on what is proportionate for your business.

What we look at

  • Your current systems and where they run
  • What the business depends on day to day
  • Security, backup and connectivity needs
  • Current costs and likely cloud costs
  • Supplier and support dependencies

What you get

  • A clear view of what is ready to move
  • What should stay where it is for now
  • Key risks and prerequisites
  • A sensible order for moving things
  • A practical next-step roadmap

No giant transformation programme.

The aim is to give you enough clarity to make a sound business decision and avoid an expensive migration that solves the wrong problem.
